Make an item

Something unique, made up on the spot. Shard / scroll: type the spell (always 1x1, can be attuned). Weapon: a sword in your color. Plain item: a tag with its first letter. Then give it to a player or put it in a stash (Give / Put here).

Characters

Reset all: everyone gets the starting kit back, both hands and feet, no afflictions or char.* flags, no attunements, and can find things again. The crew's crate (shared by every activity) goes back to its start. Activities keep their own memory (use an activity's Reset for that).

Play log

Everything players do, newest first. Kept in data/log/ (see scripts/log.js).
